# Notifications and the tray

## Notifications

Agents Hub can tell you when an agent:

- finishes,
- needs you (for example, it asks a question or needs your permission), or
- stops on an error.

It only does so while you are looking elsewhere: when the chat isn't the one on screen, or the Agents Hub window isn't the one you're using. Click the notification to open its chat.

You can have a desktop notification, a sound, or both. Turn them on or off in [Settings](settings.md), under **Notifications**, and use **Send a test** to try them.

## Keep agents running with the window closed

Turn on **Keep running in the tray** in [Settings](settings.md). Closing the window then keeps Agents Hub and your agents running, with an icon in your desktop's tray (usually at the corner of the panel or taskbar).

Click the tray icon, or choose **Show Agents Hub** in its menu, to bring the window back. The menu also has **Settings** and **Quit**.

If your desktop has no tray, closing the window quits the app.

## What happens when you quit

Quitting Agents Hub stops every agent, and everything they started. Your chats are saved, and each one picks up its conversation the next time you open it.

Opening Agents Hub while it is already running brings the existing window forward, instead of starting a second copy.
