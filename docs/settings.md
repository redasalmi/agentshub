# Settings

Open the settings by clicking the gear button at the bottom of the rail, choosing **Settings** in the tray menu, or pressing **Ctrl+,**. Press it again, or click the close button, to go back to your chat.

## Look and feel

- **Theme**: the app's colours. Click one of the previews to pick it.
- **Layout**: how the window lists your chats. **Classic** lists each project's chats beside a rail of projects. **Triage** lists every project's chats in one list, grouped by what needs you (see [The Triage layout](projects-and-chats.md#the-triage-layout)). Picking the other one asks whether to restart Agents Hub to use it:
  - **Restart now** stops the agents running now and opens Agents Hub again in the new layout, with the window where it was. Each chat picks its conversation up again when you open it.
  - **Later** keeps everything as it is, and Agents Hub opens in the new layout the next time it starts. A note under the setting says so, with its own **Restart now**.
- **Mode**: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your desktop's light or dark preference.
- **Motion**: how much the app animates. **System** follows your desktop's reduced-motion preference. **Reduced** keeps changes of colour, and slows or stills what moves.
- **Terminal text**: the size of the text in every terminal. Use **Smaller** and **Bigger**, or double-click the size to reset it. Holding **Ctrl** and scrolling over a terminal does the same.

## Behavior

- **Keep running in the tray**: closing the window keeps your agents working. See [Notifications and the tray](notifications-and-tray.md).
- **Start chats in a git worktree**: new chats in git projects get their own copy of the project. See [Separate checkouts for each chat](worktrees.md).
- **Code editor**: the editor the button at the top of a chat opens the chat's folder in. It lists the installed editors Agents Hub knows. See [Working in a chat](working-in-a-chat.md#opening-the-folder-in-your-editor).

## Notifications

- **Show a notification**: a desktop notification when an agent finishes, needs you or stops on an error while you're looking elsewhere.
- **Play a sound**: a sound at those moments.
- **Send a test**: try what you turned on.

## Keyboard

**Show** lists every keyboard shortcut. See also [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## Data: backing up and moving your chats

All your projects, chats, their history and your settings are kept in one file on your computer.

- **Export a copy** saves a copy of that file wherever you choose. Use it as a backup, or to move your chats to another computer.
- **Import…** replaces your current chats and settings with a file you exported before. Agents Hub asks you to confirm, keeps a backup of what it replaces, and restarts.

The version of Agents Hub you are running is shown at the bottom of the settings.
